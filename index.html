Electronic music written as code. Signals recovered from three worlds: a city that never sleeps, an older age of fire and stone, and the frozen, drum-lit edges of the map. Pick one, transmit it, then rewrite it while it plays.

Edit any line, then press Ctrl + Enter to re-transmit. Ctrl + . cuts the signal. Outside the editor: 1–9 pick a song, Space plays or cuts, ← → step through, R tunes to a random frequency.

Written in Strudel, the JavaScript port of TidalCycles. Also on this frequency: a step sequencer synthesised from scratch with the Web Audio API.
